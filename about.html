About This Site

ERP Web Technology Hub · erp-w.com

What this site is

erp-w.com is a bilingual (English and Chinese) technology site about building browser-based ERP systems. It documents the engineering decisions, data models, algorithms and UI patterns that come out of real ERP delivery work — the kind of knowledge that is usually scattered across project archives and hallway conversations.

The content is organized into six sections: Architecture, Finance, Manufacturing & MRP, Workflow, Integration and Enterprise UI.

Editorial stance

  • Practice over theory. Every article describes something that has been built and run, including where the naive approach fails.
  • Boring technology, clearly explained. We prefer a data model that works over an architecture diagram that impresses.
  • Bilingual by default. Every article is published in English and Chinese. English is the default language; the Chinese version lives under /zh/.

Technical choices

This site itself follows the same philosophy it preaches: pure static HTML with a single stylesheet, zero JavaScript, and anchor pagination. Pages load instantly on any connection, and there is nothing to break.
